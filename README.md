## The line

> Sorry, I heard 'call me a cab' as 'call me a crab.' You are now, legally, a crab. The cab is outside.

101 characters, one line. The mishearing is concrete (cab → crab), the twist is a flat device-voice legalism, and the kicker lands fast.
